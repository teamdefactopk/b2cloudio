Who we are

Your technology, managed end to end

Most companies run on a patchwork of providers: a domain registrar here, a hosting company there, a cloud account and email from other vendors, and a contractor in between. When something breaks, nobody owns the problem.

b2cloud.io takes ownership. We operate our own platforms where that serves you best, extend our offering through trusted white-label infrastructure partners, and manage the public clouds and software you already use. Whatever runs underneath, you deal with a single team, one agreement and one invoice.

From offices in five countries, we serve clients across the Middle East, Europe, North America and South Asia, with regional infrastructure for data residency and support that follows the sun.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we have to move our cloud accounts to you?

No. We can manage your existing AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or Oracle accounts, or provide them on our consolidated billing. You choose.

Can our data stay in our country?

Yes. We offer hosting in the UAE, UK, EU, Canada and Pakistan, and design services around your residency and compliance requirements.

What if we already have an IT team?

Choose Foundation or a co-managed arrangement. We cover out-of-hours monitoring, specialist skills or specific platforms, and your team keeps control.

How long does onboarding take?

Most single-service engagements start within a few weeks. Larger migrations follow a plan agreed during the assessment.

How are we billed?

One monthly or annual invoice in USD covering services, licences and cloud usage. You can pay by card, bank transfer, direct debit or invoice terms. Taxes apply according to your billing country.
